# Quality criteria

Use stable IDs. Record the criteria revision with each assessment. Assign a new ID when the purpose changes.

| ID | Criterion | Observable check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WORLD-01 | Physical behavior | Use the same physical rule in different situations. For example, release an object over a table and over open floor. It settles on the surface below it. |
| WORLD-02 | Continuity | Change an object, leave the area, and return. Its state reflects what happened while the user was away. |
| WORLD-03 | Human activity | Complete a familiar activity by more than one reasonable sequence. The world responds consistently and shows the consequences. |
| WORLD-04 | Social behavior | Observe a simulated person pursue a routine. Interrupt it. Check that their response fits the situation and their knowledge. |
| WORLD-05 | Connected places | Carry an object from one place to another. Check that the same rules apply and that both places retain the effects of the action. |
| WORLD-06 | Human experience | Observe first-time users. Record where their actions match real-world expectations, where they need help, and whether they choose to stay or explore. |
| ROOM-01 | Room load | Open the documented entry point. The room, table, cup, and stand are visible. |
| ROOM-02 | Movement and keyboard look | Use the documented keys. The view moves and turns as commanded. |
| ROOM-03 | Mouse look | Click the room in visible Edge. Move the mouse. The view turns. Press Esc to release the pointer. |
| ROOM-04 | Controls and status visibility | At desktop viewport sizes 800 × 480, 960 × 640, and 1280 × 720, the room, instructions, task status, and Reset button are visible without scrolling. |
| ROOM-05 | Keyboard access in a narrow viewport | Tab to the room, trigger a recovery message, tab to Reset, and press Enter. The focus outline is visible and there is no horizontal scrolling. |
| ROOM-06 | Collision | Walk into a wall and the table. The player cannot pass through either. |
| ROOM-07 | Cup pickup | Try from far away, then within reach. The cup stays on the table on the first attempt and moves with the player on the second. |
| ROOM-08 | Cup placement | Put the cup on the stand and walk away. The cup stays on the stand and the completion status remains visible. |
| ROOM-09 | Recovery after a failed action | Try pickup and placement from too far away, then while facing away within reach. The message identifies the correction and tells the user which key to press again. |
| ROOM-10 | Reset | Reset after placement. The player and cup return to their initial positions and the task is incomplete. |
| ROOM-11 | Willingness to stay | After the cup task, let first-time human users continue or leave. Record voluntary active time, including zero, separately from any time or reasons they volunteer. Use the first human assessment as a baseline. Compare later results under similar conditions. |
