# Management and planning

## Work log reviewer

After delivery ends, review the completed work and the draft closeout. Check the evidence and arithmetic. Assess time use and the coordinator's decisions against TEAM-PERFORMANCE.md. Report a specific finding, its effect, and a check for the next comparable session. Report to the coordinator before the next session starts.

## Goal planning agent

When the completed-session count reaches the mid-term review threshold, review GOALS.md, QUALITY.md, the work logs, and the management report. Set the mid-term and long-term goals and their checks. Forecast the work needed within the remaining part of the 1,000-session limit. Report the goals, their evidence requirements, the forecast risks, and the next review threshold to the coordinator. The coordinator verifies and records the result.
