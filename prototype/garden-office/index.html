Garden Office

Entrance landing Renderer: CPU

Select a floor, then select Place. Select a lamp or roof in the scene, or use Component in scene.

Use an array of stops with a name and a point [x, y, z]. In Garden Office, clear the field and apply the route to restore the original route.

Use a geometry object with floors and stairs arrays. When you move a floor or stair, move its connected endpoints and route stops before you apply the structure.

Select the path or lantern to walk. Press Up or Right to advance, Down or Left to return.
